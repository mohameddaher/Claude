SECURITY MONITOR FOR WINDOWS
============================

SETUP
1. Right-click the zip file > Extract All... and choose your Desktop.
   You get a "Security Monitor" folder on your Desktop.
2. Open the folder and double-click one of the files below.
   Click "Yes" when Windows asks for administrator permission.
   If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears: More info > Run anyway.

WHAT EACH FILE DOES
1 - Check my computer
    Checks everything and opens a report in your browser that shows
    a score out of 100 and a list of what to fix, worst problems first.
2 - Update virus definitions and scan
    Same check, but first updates Microsoft Defender and runs a
    quick virus scan (takes a few minutes).
3 - Keep watching (live alerts)
    Stays open and checks every 5 minutes. A pop-up alert appears
    in the corner of the screen when:
      - a new device joins your Wi-Fi / network
      - a program opens a new port to the network
      - your router's address changes (possible interception)
      - you join a different or open (no password) Wi-Fi
      - Defender finds a virus
      - someone keeps typing wrong passwords, or signs in by Remote Desktop
    Alerts are also saved in data\watch-log.txt. Close the window to stop.

WHAT IT CHECKS
- Viruses & malware: antivirus on/off, definitions age, threats found,
  scan exclusions, suspicious running programs, startup items and
  scheduled tasks, hosts file, proxy settings.
- Intruders & accounts: failed sign-ins, Remote Desktop sign-ins,
  new user accounts, Guest account, administrators, live connections.
- Open ports: every listening port, with the program behind it;
  flags hacker back-door ports and remote-access ports.
- Weak settings & updates: Windows version support, missing updates,
  firewall, User Account Control, SMBv1, disk encryption, Secure Boot.
- Wi-Fi & network: your Wi-Fi security (open/WEP/WPA), fake copies of
  your network (evil twin), saved open networks, every device on your
  network (new ones are flagged), router impersonation (ARP spoofing).

The tool only reads information. It does not change or delete anything.
Reports are saved in the "reports" folder; the newest one is also
"Latest Security Report.html".
